How I use this page

I read every saved kit, tip, ability value and new ability from here when you tell me you're done. A changed kit is marked experiment, and each changed slot gets an accent bar.

Allow other classes' moves opens the whole pool of active abilities to that specialization. Death Mark and Peck normally come only with a kit, but here you can put them in any slot. The Abilities section below the kits holds the ability values (closed: a cooldown summary; open: every number to edit), the passives and the box for new ones.
